Captain Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Heartifo may collect, use, store, share, and protect information relating to Captains who use the Heartifo Captain platform.

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the Heartifo Captain App, we may collect information such as:

  • Name and profile information.
  • Mobile number and account information.
  • Profile photograph.
  • Identity and verification information.
  • Driving licence information.
  • Vehicle registration and vehicle details.
  • Documents submitted for verification.
  • Ride, trip, pickup and destination information.
  • Location information while using relevant Captain features.
  • Payment and earnings-related information.
  • Customer ratings, reviews, and support communications.
  • Device and technical information.

2. Identity and Document Information

Heartifo may collect documents required for Captain registration, eligibility, identity verification, vehicle verification, or compliance with applicable requirements.

These may include identity documents, driving licence, vehicle registration documents, photographs, and other required information.

Heartifo aims to collect and retain only information that is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose and applicable requirements.

3. Location Information

The Captain App may use location information to support features such as receiving ride requests, navigating to pickup locations, tracking rides, calculating relevant trip information, improving service operations, and supporting safety investigations.

Location access may depend on the permissions you provide and the features you use.

4. How We Use Information

Heartifo may use Captain information to:

  • Create and manage Captain accounts.
  • Verify Captain and vehicle information.
  • Connect Captains with Customers.
  • Facilitate and manage rides.
  • Calculate and display applicable earnings.
  • Process relevant payments and payouts.
  • Provide customer and Captain support.
  • Improve platform performance and reliability.
  • Prevent fraud, misuse, and security incidents.
  • Investigate complaints and safety incidents.
  • Meet applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

5. Information Shared With Customers

During a relevant ride, certain Captain information may be shown to the Customer to help identify the Captain and vehicle.

Depending on the service, this may include information such as Captain name, profile photograph, rating, vehicle information, and relevant ride details.

6. Service Providers

Heartifo may use trusted third-party service providers to support services such as hosting, authentication, communications, payments, maps, analytics, storage, verification, security, and customer support.

Such providers may process information only as necessary to provide their services or as otherwise permitted by applicable law.

7. Document Storage and Security

Captain documents may be stored using secure storage systems. Access may be restricted to authorized personnel or systems that require the information for legitimate business, verification, safety, or compliance purposes.

Heartifo takes reasonable measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

No online system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Captains should also protect their account credentials and authentication information.

8. Payment and Earnings Information

Information relating to Captain earnings, payouts, applicable fees, adjustments, and transactions may be processed to operate the Heartifo platform and provide relevant financial services.

9. Ratings and Reviews

Customer ratings and feedback may be associated with a Captain account and used for platform operations, service improvement, safety reviews, and enforcement of applicable policies.

10. Safety and Fraud Prevention

Heartifo may process account, ride, location, device, and other relevant information to detect and investigate fraud, suspicious activity, safety concerns, policy violations, and misuse of the platform.

11. Data Retention

Heartifo may retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including providing services, maintaining records, resolving disputes, preventing fraud, meeting legal obligations, and protecting users.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and applicable requirements.

12. Your Choices

Depending on applicable law and the type of information, you may have rights or choices relating to your personal information.

You may also manage certain device permissions, including location permissions, through your device settings. Some Heartifo features may not function correctly if required permissions are disabled.

13. Account Deletion

A Captain may request account deletion by contacting Heartifo Support.

Some information may need to be retained for legal, regulatory, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, financial, or other legitimate purposes even after an account deletion request.

14. Children's Privacy

The Heartifo Captain platform is intended for eligible individuals who meet the applicable requirements for providing transportation services. We do not knowingly provide Captain accounts to children.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Heartifo may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, technology, legal requirements, or data practices.

Updated versions will be published on this page.
